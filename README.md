# Blinkit-Dashboard
1. Project Title / Headline

Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard

A dynamic and interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, customer behavior, and outlet operations through data-driven visualizations and business insights.

2. Short Description / Purpose

The Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard is a visually appealing and analytical Power BI report designed to provide comprehensive insights into Blinkit's retail sales data. The dashboard highlights key business metrics such as Total Sales ($1.20M), Average Sales ($141), Number of Sales (8,523), and Average Customer Rating (3.9). It enables users to explore sales performance across different outlet locations, outlet sizes, outlet types, and product categories. The report helps identify sales trends, customer preferences, and operational patterns, supporting data-driven decision-making and business growth strategies.

3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Main platform used for dashboard development and visualization.
🔄 Power Query – Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Creation of calculated measures and KPIs.
🗂️ Data Modeling – Establishing relationships between tables for efficient analysis.
📈 Interactive Visuals – KPI Cards, Donut Charts, Bar Charts, Line Charts, Slicers, and Tables.
📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard previews.

4. Data Source

The dataset contains Blinkit retail sales information, including outlet details, product categories, item visibility, customer ratings, sales transactions, and outlet establishment data. The data was processed and transformed to generate meaningful business insights and performance metrics.

5. Features / Highlights
• Business Problem

Blinkit handles a large volume of sales transactions across multiple outlet types, locations, and product categories. Analyzing this data manually makes it difficult to identify sales trends, customer preferences, and outlet performance. The business requires a centralized analytics solution to monitor key metrics and support data-driven decision-making.

Key Questions Such As:
Which outlet type generates the highest sales revenue?
Which product categories contribute the most to total sales?
How does outlet size impact sales performance?
Which outlet locations perform better than others?
What are the customer satisfaction levels across outlets?
How have sales trends changed over different establishment years?
• Goal of the Dashboard

The primary goal of this dashboard is to provide a comprehensive overview of Blinkit's sales performance, customer ratings, and outlet operations. It helps stakeholders identify growth opportunities, optimize inventory management, improve customer satisfaction, and enhance business profitability.

Discovered the most profitable product segments.
Evaluated customer preferences based on fat content and product categories.
Compared sales performance across outlet sizes and locations.
Supported strategic business decisions through data-driven insights.
Improved understanding of customer behavior and retail performance trends.
